How Guardian Linking Works

  • The Care Seeker can view their approval code in their dashboard header (e.g. CS-8842).
  • Once linked, you will receive real-time notifications for daily medicine adherence, task progress, and SOS alerts.
  • You can link multiple relatives (e.g. both father and mother) to a single guardian account.
  • You may disconnect or unlink anytime if care responsibilities change.
